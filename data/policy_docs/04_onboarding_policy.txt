TechNova Solutions - Employee Onboarding Policy

New employees must complete onboarding within their first 5 working days.
Employees must submit identity proof, address proof and educational certificates.
IT must provide the assigned laptop and company email account by the first working day.
New employees must complete information security training within 10 working days.
Managers must assign an onboarding buddy to every new employee.
Probation lasts for 6 months unless the employment offer states otherwise.
