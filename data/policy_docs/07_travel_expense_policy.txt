TechNova Solutions - Travel Expense Reimbursement Policy

Employees can claim approved transportation expenses during business travel.
Taxi expenses are reimbursed up to 1500 INR per day with receipts.
Personal sightseeing and entertainment expenses are not reimbursable.
Expense claims require manager approval.
Claims submitted after 30 calendar days may be rejected.
Original receipts or valid digital receipts must be attached.
