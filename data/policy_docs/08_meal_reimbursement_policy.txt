TechNova Solutions - Meal Reimbursement Policy

Employees travelling overnight for business may claim meal expenses.
Breakfast reimbursement is limited to 200 INR per day.
Lunch reimbursement is limited to 350 INR per day.
Dinner reimbursement is limited to 450 INR per day.
Meal claims require itemized receipts.
Alcohol and personal guest expenses are not reimbursable.
