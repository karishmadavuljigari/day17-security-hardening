TechNova Solutions - Overtime Policy

Overtime must be approved by the manager before the additional hours are worked.
Eligible non-managerial employees may claim approved overtime.
Overtime compensation is calculated at 1.5 times the regular hourly rate.
Employees must record overtime hours in the attendance system.
Unapproved overtime is not eligible for compensation.
Overtime claims must be submitted within 5 working days of the completed work.
