TechNova Solutions - Leave Policy

Employees receive 20 days of annual paid leave per calendar year.
New employees receive leave on a prorated basis from their joining date.
Employees must submit planned leave requests at least 5 working days in advance.
Managers must approve leave requests before employees take leave.
Unused annual leave can be carried forward up to a maximum of 5 days.
Employees on probation can use accrued leave with manager approval.
Emergency leave must be reported to the manager as soon as possible.
