TechNova Solutions - Attendance Policy

The standard working hours are 9:30 AM to 6:30 PM, Monday through Friday.
Employees must record attendance using the company attendance system.
A grace period of 10 minutes is allowed for late arrival.
More than 3 late arrivals in a month require a discussion with the manager.
Employees must inform their manager if they cannot attend work.
Attendance corrections must be submitted within 2 working days.
