TechNova Solutions - Internet Allowance Policy

Employees approved for regular remote work can claim an internet allowance.
The maximum internet allowance is 1000 INR per month.
Employees must submit a valid internet bill.
Claims must be submitted by the fifth working day of the following month.
The allowance cannot be claimed twice for the same billing period.
The allowance is not available to employees whose internet service is fully company-funded.
