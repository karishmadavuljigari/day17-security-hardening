TechNova Solutions - Remote Work Policy

Eligible employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week.
Employees must obtain manager approval for their remote work schedule.
Employees working remotely must remain available during core hours from 10 AM to 4 PM.
Company data must only be accessed through approved devices and secure connections.
Employees must attend mandatory in-person meetings when requested.
Remote work privileges may be reviewed based on business requirements.
